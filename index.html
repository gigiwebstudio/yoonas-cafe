Our Story

A café that never chose sides.

Yoona's opened on Robson Street as two ideas at once — the neighbourhood breakfast spot people stop into before work, and the tapas room they come back to after it. Rather than pick one, we built a menu that changes with the light outside.

Mornings mean scramblers, benedicts, and a slow pour of coffee. Evenings shift toward Korean-style mandu, hand-pressed smash burgers, and a pasta corner that leans Roman. Same room, same warmth — different rhythm.
